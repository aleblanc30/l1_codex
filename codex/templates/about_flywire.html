Data Sources
  • Cell reconstructions are skeletons traced from electron-microscopy images in CATMAID
  • Cell reconstructions were traced by the L1 community (see L1)
  • Synaptic connections are the connectors annotated in the L1 CATMAID project
  • Annotations (side, cell type and publications) are taken from the L1 CATMAID project
  • Neurotransmitter types are not yet determined
  • Morphological similarity scores (NBLAST based) are not yet computed
